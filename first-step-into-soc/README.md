# First Step into SOC

## Objective

Complete the TryHackMe "First Step into SOC" room and learn the basics of Security Operations Center (SOC) work, including:

- Human attack vectors
- Software vulnerabilities
- Supply chain attacks
- Incident response
- Basic remediation planning

---

## Skills Practiced

- Security awareness
- Incident triage
- Vulnerability management
- Patch management
- Basic threat analysis
- SOC decision making

---

## Key Concepts

### Human Attacks

Examples include:

- weak passwords
- phishing
- malicious USB devices
- social engineering

---

### Software Vulnerabilities

Learned how software vulnerabilities receive CVE identifiers and why timely patching is essential.

Examples discussed:

- PrintNightmare
- EternalBlue
- Follina
- ToolShell

---

### Supply Chain Attacks

Learned how trusted software or libraries may become compromised.

Examples:

- SolarWinds
- 3CX

---

## Practical Lab

During the lab I investigated multiple vulnerable systems and selected the most appropriate remediation actions.

Examples included:

- applying security patches
- restricting access to exposed services
- implementing antivirus protection
- reducing attack surface

The exercise emphasized prioritizing permanent remediation (patching) over temporary workarounds whenever possible.

---

## Lessons Learned

- Patch management is one of the most effective security controls.
- Human error remains one of the largest attack vectors.
- Supply chain attacks can affect trusted software.
- Vulnerability management requires both mitigation and detection.
- A SOC analyst should focus on reducing risk while maintaining business continuity.
